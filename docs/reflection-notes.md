## Creative framing: the director's commentary

Use the DVD theme in the writing without losing the reflection's substance. Three short sections could be titled **Choosing the audience**, **Behind the scenes**, and **The next take**. Keep the reflection in the Brightspace assignment text area rather than creating a public portfolio chapter.

### Choosing the audience

- Who specifically is the chatbot artifact for, and what does that audience need?
- What did you change about the artifact itself for that audience: examples, terminology, instructions, presentation, or level of detail?
- Why is the chatbot useful to that audience? Point to the actual deliverable rather than only the portfolio's TV theme.
- The portfolio's Dr. Collins profile prioritizes coursework, while the recruiter profile prioritizes earlier project evidence. Explain this distinction only if it is relevant to your reflection.

### Behind the scenes

- What did you personally build, test, or revise within the team?
- What surprising output, failed prompt, limitation, or tradeoff changed your thinking?
- Which concrete before-and-after example shows your learning?
- How does this connect with your value proposition of making complex ideas approachable through usable software?

### The next take

- What real feedback did a teammate, instructor, or user provide?
- What did you change in response, and why?
- If you have not received feedback, say so accurately and name the feedback you intend to request. Do not invent a review.
- What would you test or improve next?
